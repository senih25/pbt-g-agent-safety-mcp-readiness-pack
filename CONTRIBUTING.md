# Contributing

## Public-safe contribution rule

Contribute only high-level guidance, synthetic examples, or documentation that can be safely published. Do not submit proprietary code, secrets, private paths, internal policy details, customer data, or unapproved product claims.

## Contribution process

1. Open a proposal using synthetic examples and the smallest necessary context.
2. State the intended audience, risk addressed, and any claim requiring evidence.
3. Check links, spelling, and whether the text could reveal implementation details.
4. Obtain maintainer approval before merging or publishing.

## Content standards

- Use clear, non-alarmist language.
- Avoid promises of complete protection, compliance, or certification.
- Mark assumptions and verification steps.
- Keep examples fictional and non-destructive.
- Add a changelog entry for user-visible changes.

## Review gate

Maintainers must confirm the material belongs to the public documentation pack and does not overlap with private PBT-G source, runtime operations, or evidence archives.

# Privacy

## Public-pack orientation

This pack is designed for local review and synthetic demonstrations. It does not require raw customer secrets, production tokens, or private-repository access.

## Data minimization

Keep review notes limited to what is necessary: a tool category, a risk pattern, an owner, a remediation decision, and a safe evidence reference. Remove private paths, account identifiers, request bodies, and screenshots containing sensitive data.

## Collection

The documentation itself performs no data collection. Any future hosted or commercial offering must publish its own approved privacy notice, data-processing terms, retention rules, and support process before collection begins.

## Commercial terms placeholder

Commercial terms and a commercial privacy notice are not included in this public pack. They require separate owner, legal, and product approval.
